Before You Travel

A little preparation can make a trip much easier.

  1. Check your passport and travel documents.
  2. Book your hotel and transport in advance.
  3. Check the weather at your destination.
  4. Make a list of important things to take.

What to Pack

  • Comfortable clothes
  • Travel documents
  • Phone and charger
  • Personal items

Travel Tips for Our Destinations

Plan Ahead

Research your destination before travelling. Check transportation, accommodation and important places you would like to visit.

Keep Your Important Items Safe

Keep your passport, money and other important documents in a safe place during your trip.
